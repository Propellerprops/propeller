PROPELLER
AI Rename Tool — name items from their photos

Step 1 — Load your inventory

This pulls your live items (ID, current name, photo) straight from your sheet. Dimensions and every other field are left untouched — this tool only proposes new names.

Auto-load blocked? Open this link in a normal browser tab, copy everything, and paste it below:

propeller-app.netlify.app/.netlify/functions/api?action=getItems

If it keeps timing out, just use the paste box — show it now.

Step 2 — Generate names from photos

Claude looks at each item's photo and fills in a clear catalogue name, a subcategory (Sofa, Armchair, Shelving…), and an era / style (Mid-Century, Art Deco…). Dimensions are never touched. Only items with a photo are processed; you can edit anything afterwards.

Step 3 — Review & export

Check the suggested names, tweak anything, then copy. The rows are in the same order as your sheet, so you can paste the new-name column straight into column B (starting at B3).
